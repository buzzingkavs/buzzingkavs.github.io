About

I spent four years asking why a honey bee stings. Now I spend my time making that kind of question legible to people who don't have four years.

I hold a PhD in biology from the University of Konstanz, where I studied defensive behaviour and neuroethology in honey bees, and an MSc in neuroscience from the EUCOR joint programme in Strasbourg, Freiburg, and Basel. I write and illustrate science for general audiences, and I'm based in Strasbourg.

I'm currently looking for work in scientific publishing, editorial, science communication, and medical communications. If you're commissioning, hiring, or just want to talk about insects, write to me.

Tools — Adobe Illustrator, Affinity Designer, R, Python, LaTeX
Languages — English (C2), Tamil (native), German (conversational), French (basic)
