About

I hold a PhD in biology from the University of Konstanz, where I studied defensive behaviour and neuroethology in honey bees, and an MSc in neuroscience from the EUCOR joint programme in Strasbourg, Freiburg, and Basel. I write science for general audiences, and I illustrate my own work when the subject asks for it. I'm based in Strasbourg.

I'm looking for work in scientific publishing, editorial, science communication, and medical communications. If you're commissioning or hiring, write to me.
